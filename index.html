Abstract

This document serves as a repository for various links that can lead to the eventual safe archival and mirroring of the first five entries of the now-discontinued Pick-A-Photo stock photo software provided by Ulead Systems in the early 2000's.

These photos were mostly known amongst some netizens as part of the assets of the first few games of the Windows-era Touhou Project games developed by ZUN.

A few individuals have expressed interest in tracking down and obtaining copies of these software. Some have even compiled their own lists, but either have also been unable to find the images or gatekeep them, for understandable reasons.

Despite the ever-growing presence of the online Touhou community, these images are nearly guaranteed to become nothing more than mere trivia and a handful of low-resolution samples worthy of a creepypasta.

The ultimate goal of this document is to spread enough awareness such that there will be a high enough chance for someone to re-upload a compressed archive of these images to a reliable and accessible domain (such as the Internet Archive), such that in case even if an unfortunate event happens, manmade or not, to said domain, there will be enough copies saved by private individuals such that a speedy reupload is mathematically possible.

This document used to be hosted on Google Docs, but has transitioned into static HTML page, hosted on a GitHub and Codeberg repository, licensed such that even if the images themselves cannot be distributed, the knowledge and awareness can spread.

We welcome all suggestions and additions to this document.

Arplane's Archive & bilibili videos

A user named Arplane has archived Ulead Pick-a-Photo 05: Oriental Antiques and Customs.

  • Pick-a-Photo 05

The existing archive contains the elusive images of the pack; however, they are only available in 1062x754, which is the second-highest resolution size.

The maximum resolution is 3720x2640, which has been archived for all other packs in the series. In a review, mister_fumo has requested that Arplane upload the highest-quality versions of the images.

The author has inquired about this situation, and response from Arplane was:

“because I only asked if this size can be uploaded”

As such, please refrain from bothering Arplane.
